# Weekly update policy

## Eligible for automatic publication

Only when supported by current primary or clearly reliable sources and all tests pass:

- official price or trim changes;
- official promotion or finance offer start, change or expiry;
- official warranty and aftersales changes;
- new monthly or year-to-date sales records with a comparable scope;
- dated official marketing activities;
- correction of a source URL, publication date or clearly factual label;
- national policy text that can be stated without making a new vehicle-specific customs conclusion.

## Always hold for human review

- add, remove, adopt or re-tier a competitor;
- apply a tariff, FTA, origin rule or exemption to a specific vehicle without direct customs/import evidence;
- resolve conflicting official sources where the business conclusion changes;
- change schema, field meaning, page structure, website code, branding or design;
- delete historical evidence rather than expire it;
- publish an estimate as a confirmed value;
- change GitHub, Vercel, domain, secrets or schedule configuration.

## No-change run

A no-change result is valid. Record searched channels, policy/customs checks, expired-item checks and the current deployment SHA. Do not create a meaningless commit.
