About

/ Profile

I design and build at the seam between intelligence and interface — where models become products people actually use.

I'm interested in how AI changes the shape of products — not as a feature bolted on, but as a new material for thinking, deciding and creating. My work lives in that uncertain, fast-moving space.

Much of my time goes into product design and engineering: sketching systems, writing the first lines, and shipping things that hold up under real use. I care about restraint — clear structure over decoration, speed over spectacle.

The future I'm building toward is quieter and more useful than the hype suggests: tools that respect attention, data that stays legible, and interfaces that feel like architecture rather than noise.
